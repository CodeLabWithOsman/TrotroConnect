## 🎯 The Problem

Every day, **3.5 million trotro trips** happen in Greater Accra with:

- ❌ **Zero real-time tracking** - Passengers wait blindly
- ❌ **No arrival time estimates** - Could be 5 minutes or 50 minutes
- ❌ **No fare transparency** - Frequent disputes and overcharging
- ⏱️ **20-45 minute average wait times** with no information

**70% of urban commuters** depend on this system.  
**0%** have visibility into when their trotro will arrive.

### The Cost

| Passengers | Drivers |
|------------|---------|
| ⏱️ 2-3 hours wasted daily | ⛽ ₵900/month wasted fuel |
| 😰 Stress & uncertainty | 🔄 Inefficient routing |
| 📚 Students miss classes | 💸 Lost revenue potential |
| 👔 Workers late to jobs | 😓 14+ hour work days |

> *"I spend 3 hours daily just waiting and guessing. Nobody knows when the trotro will come."*  
> — Ama, Daily Commuter, Madina Station (Survey respondent, Jan 2026)

## ✨ The Solution

Trotro Connect brings **Uber-level transparency** to Ghana's existing trotro network—without disrupting drivers or replacing the system.

### Core Features

#### 📍 Real-Time GPS Tracking
- See your trotro approaching **live on the map**
- 30-second location updates
- Accurate **ETA calculations**
- Works on any smartphone browser

#### 💰 GPRTU Fare Calculator
- **Official May 2025 approved fares**
- No more fare disputes
- Transparent pricing for every route
- GPRTU-verified accuracy

#### 🗺️ Interactive Map
- Street view and satellite view toggle
- Live driver locations
- Passenger location marker
- Route visualization

### How to Test the Prototype

#### Passenger Experience:
1. **Search** for a destination (try: "Madina", "Tema", or "Kasoa")
2. **See** the nearest trotro with real-time location
3. **Track** the vehicle live on the map
4. **View** official GPRTU fare for the route

#### Driver Experience:
1. **Tap** "GO ACTIVE" to start broadcasting location
2. **Drive** your normal route (no distractions)
3. **GPS** auto-broadcasts to passengers
4. **View** earnings and activity feed
